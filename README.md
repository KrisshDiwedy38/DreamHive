# 🌐 DreamHive: Social + Productivity Platform

**DreamHive** is a multifunctional digital platform designed to empower entrepreneurs, innovators, and dreamers by providing a one-stop hub for managing projects, ideas, and collaborations.

By blending **social networking** with **productivity tools**, DreamHive helps you streamline workflows, share your journey, and connect with a vibrant community of like-minded individuals.

---

## ✨ Highlights

- 🚀 **One-stop productivity + social platform**  
- 🔐 **JWT-based authentication** for secure access  
- 📊 **Goal tracking & productivity boards** to organize work  
- 🤝 **Collaboration features** with real-time updates  
- 💬 **Community-driven engagement** with posts, comments, and progress feeds  
- 🎮 **Gamified progress tracking** to enhance motivation  

---

## 🛠 Tech Stack

- **Backend:** Django, PostgreSQL, Redis, JWT, WebSockets  
- **Frontend:** React, Typescript, CSS
